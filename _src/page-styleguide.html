The foil

Not a colour: a material built from three golds, with the light stop in the middle so it falls away like a real foil stamp. The most distinctive device the brand owns.

Vertical

Display type. The vertical ramp is what reads as metal.

Horizontal

Buttons. The highlight travels on hover.

Vertical, on dark

Brightened so it holds against ink.

Tag

Chips and flags. Short ramp so it never bands small.

  • Stops: #8A6A1C low, #C29A2B mid, #EDD27C high. Never invent a fourth.
  • On white: foil is allowed as one accent word inside a display headline. The ink words carry the sentence; the gold word is the flourish.
  • Body-size foil on white is banned. The ramp peaks too light to read.
  • Small type on gold is only legal on the tag ramp, in ink.

Type: one family, two voices

Everything is Archivo, a variable font. The display voice is condensed (width 62), uppercase, weight 800, line-height .85. The reading voice is normal width. There is no second typeface anywhere.

Bands, layout, and the seam rules

It's better to be lucky.

The green frame
  • Green is chrome: the sticky masthead with its 2px gold rule, and brand punctuation
  • White type only. Gold appears on green as the rule or the clover, never as words
  • Page rhythm: green masthead, ink hero, white content with cream cards, one ink moment mid-page, ink closing call, ink footer with the gold rule. Every template follows it.
  • Radius law: small surfaces (buttons, chips, inputs) take 6px. Large surfaces (cards, tiles, photographs) take 14px. A corner-anchored chip rounds only its inner corner.
  • The seam rule: background emblems crop only at the viewport edge or at a colour change, never at an invisible same-colour seam. Adjacent white sections are one canvas; an emblem may span them.
  • Rails: every horizontal scroll rail carries scroll-padding-left so snap never eats the page gutter.
  • Container: content lives in .wrap, max 1560px with fluid padding. Full-bleed moments escape it deliberately, one edge at a time.
  • Groove texture sits on every band: cream-alpha lines on dark, ink-alpha on light. It is the surface finish, not a decoration to add or remove per section.

Motion

  • Two curves only: --ease-out-s for anything entering or reacting, --ease-io-s for on-screen movement. Never ease-in on UI.
  • Buttons press (scale .975), arrows nudge 4px on hover, cards lift 4px with a tinted shadow.
  • Sections reveal on scroll (.rv), grids cascade at 60ms steps. Decorative only, content is interactive from frame one.
  • One ceremony per page: the hero load-in. Photo settles from a 4.5 percent zoom, copy rises in sequence. Seen once per visit.
  • Reduced motion kills all of it. Non-negotiable, already wired globally.
  • Hover states are gated behind (hover:hover) so touch never misfires.

Content rules that never bend

Voice lives in How We Write v8. These are the site-side rules any surface must obey regardless of who writes it.

  • No fabricated reviews, names or numbers. Reviews come from Judge.me. Order counts round down, never up: 30,000+ is honest, 35,000+ is a lie today.
  • The sixty-day trial is not on-course. Write "sixty days to decide" or "sixty days to prove us wrong". Never "play it on real grass" next to the trial.
  • "Tour-quality construction" is the permitted phrasing. "Tour-proven" is banned without a tour player.
  • No warranty language. Lucky has no warranty; the sixty-day return is the promise.
  • Placeholders are labelled shot briefs, never stock photos, never renders pretending to be photography.
  • No em dashes. Anywhere. This document included.

Email: the system, flattened

Emails should read as a simplified page of the site: same four colours, same voice, same green masthead and gold button. Flattened, because email clients cannot render the devices. Outlook uses Word's engine, Gmail clips at 102KB, and dark mode inverts colours you did not choose.

Ads and organic: the system, rendered to pixels

Creatives are images and video, so the full device set is available again: foil, grooves, the condensed display voice. The rules travel with them.

  • Grounds: ink or white, exactly like the site. Green appears as a frame element (a top bar, the clover), never as the field behind gold type.
  • Type: Archivo, condensed uppercase 800 for the one headline, sentence case for support lines. One headline per asset.
  • Gold is the product. If the club is in frame, the foil stays off the type so they never compete. No club in frame, foil headline allowed.
  • Photography: real product, gold catching real light. No renders passed off as photos, no stock golfers holding other brands.
  • The clover is the watermark and the bullet. Brand green on light, cream or gold on dark. Crop it at the frame edge, never mid-shape against a flat field.
  • Claims follow the content rules above: real review quotes with names, verified numbers, trial wording exact. An ad is not exempt.
  • Safe areas: build 4:5 first, protect the centre 1:1, keep type inside 9:16 safe zones for placement reuse.
  • Organic posts are the light version: white ground, ink type, one gold accent, clover sign-off. Photo-led posts carry the stamp device for spec callouts.

Updating this page

This page is built from the same stylesheet as the site, so visual changes to the site show up here automatically. The written specs are maintained by hand:

  1. Edit _src/page-styleguide.html (specs and examples) or _src/page-styleguide.css (this page's own chrome).
  2. Run python tools/build.py
  3. Commit and push. The page rebuilds at 90-styleguide.html.
