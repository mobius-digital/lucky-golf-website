The rules

Six devices, and the laws around them

These are what make a page read as Lucky rather than as a well-built page. Carry all six before polishing anything.

1 · Foil

Gold as a material

One display word per page plus the primary button. Never body size. On white the ramp is weighted low; on a dark band it is brightened.

2 · Mark

Mags and the clover

Mags the magpie on the bag button, the sign-off, the 404 and the empty bag. The clover lives inside the logo. Folklore says magpies steal shiny things: write "legend has it".

3 · Groove

The texture on every band

1px lines on a 6px pitch over the header, the reel and the footer. Section dividers on the page are one hairline, never three.

4 · Stamp

Condensed caps labels

Width 78, weight 600, tracked. Eyebrows, chips, tags, trust rows, table keys. Gold-as-text for the eyebrow, never gold on green.

5 · Aside

The charm voice

Sentence case, medium, one line. "Nobody needs a gold wedge. That is rather the point." Attitude with receipts; never a flat double statement.

6 · Eyebrow

The gold rule

A 22px rule and a condensed caps label lead every section. It orients, it never restates the headline.

Honesty

No fabricated anything

Reviews are verbatim Judge.me with a real name; the build refuses a quote it cannot find in the pull. Counts round down. A photo that does not exist is a labelled slot.

Motion

Still

The foil slides on hover, the film plays, Gold and Black crossfade. No scroll reveals, no lifts, no drop shadows. Reduced motion kills everything.

Touch

44 pixels

Every target on a phone is 44px tall. Galleries swipe with dots, grids become rails, the sticky bar follows the buy button off the screen and hides at the footer.
