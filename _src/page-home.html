Why it says Lucky

You're going to be looking down at this club a lot

Golf hands out the good bounce and the bad one and never explains itself. You can practise for ten years and still need the ball to kick right, which is why every golfer is superstitious about something.

So a lot of it comes down to what's in your head over the ball. And there's a real difference between looking down at a name you bought because everyone else bought it, and looking down at one that says Lucky.

One of those is a receipt. The other one is a mindset, and it reads the same whether the finish is gold or blacked out, and whether it's the wedge, the putter, or whatever goes in the bag next.

You found something most people walk straight past, you didn't overpay for it, and you know how it's built — so the confidence is earned rather than borrowed. That's worth strokes, and it never shows up on a spec sheet.
