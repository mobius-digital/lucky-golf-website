Why it says Lucky

You're going to be looking down at this club a lot

Golf is luck sometimes. The good bounce, the bad kick, the putt that lips out for no reason. Every golfer knows it. That's the name on the club.

And when you're standing over the ball, you're either looking down at the same wedge everyone else bought, or at one that says Lucky: the same tour-quality build as the big names, without the overpaid logo.

That's the double meaning. Confidence over the ball, and the quiet satisfaction of knowing you didn't pay extra for it.
