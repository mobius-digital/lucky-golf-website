Warranty and returns

Sixty days, no argument

Sixty days, with conditions

Sixty days from delivery on every club. Contact us before sending anything back — club returns need approval and photo verification first, and a club returned without authorization will not be accepted.

Refunds are issued after the club comes back and is inspected. Return shipping is the customer's, and original shipping is not refunded.

What you can do with it first

Putters may be used during the sixty days, but must come back in brand-new condition — no face marks, sole wear, scratches, dents, chips, bag chatter or grip wear. No regripping, bending, cutting or altering.

Wedges: one wedge per order may be opened and tested, on a turf mat or another non-abrasive surface. Any others stay factory sealed. The tested one must come back with no groove wear, face marks or sole wear.

Hybrids must appear completely unused, with no visible wear and no modifications.

Wear beyond these conditions can mean a partial refund or a declined return.

Apparel and gear

Thirty days. Apparel unworn and unwashed with all original tags attached — a return without tags has $7 deducted. Gear unused and undamaged.

Customised products are final sale unless they arrive defective.

Delivery

When it turns up

USPSUPSFedEx

Orders leave the warehouse in two to three business days. Ground delivery then takes three to five business days depending on where you are, and you get a tracking number as soon as the carrier scans it.

US shipping is free over $125. Below that it is charged by weight, from $8. Express (UPS 2 Day) is available at checkout, and we ship worldwide.

Where it ships from

We ship from Long Beach, California, and worldwide. Needs confirming — whether duties and import taxes are prepaid at checkout or collected on delivery.
