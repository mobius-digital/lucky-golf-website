Refund policy

Thirty days, tags on

Apparel comes back within thirty days of delivery, unworn, unwashed and in original condition with all original tags attached.

No makeup or deodorant marks, odors, pet hair, stretching or laundering. A return without its tags has $7 deducted from the refund.

Requests after thirty days are not accepted. Clubs get sixty days; apparel does not.

Wrong size

Size is the most common reason anything comes back, which is why the size guide is worth thirty seconds before you order.

Refunds are issued after the item is received and inspected. Return shipping is the customer's, and original shipping is not refunded.

Customized products are final sale unless they arrive defective. Read the full refund policy
