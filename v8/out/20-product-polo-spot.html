The polo

Same shirt. Sharper collar.

The Blade drops the buttons. No placket means nothing at your throat to sit crooked halfway round, and the collar lies flat instead of curling up on you by the ninth.

Underneath it's the Classic, unchanged: tailored athletic through the body, 88/12 poly-spandex, four-way stretch that moves at the top of the backswing. UPF 50+ and moisture-wicking, same as everything else we make.

So you're really choosing a collar and a print. That's it. Everything that matters to how it plays is identical either way.

How it fits

Tailored athletic, and the same block as the Classic. If you know your size in one, it's your size in the other.

The blade collar has no buttons and no placket, so the neckline lies flat instead of gapping. It reads a little more fitted at the shoulders for that reason alone; the body hasn't changed.

Between sizes, or want room across the chest? Size up. Returns are thirty days, unworn with the tags on.

Garment measurements, laid flat, not body measurements. Chest is measured across the chest and doubled. If you're between sizes or you like more room across the chest, take the larger one.
