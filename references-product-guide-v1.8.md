Return policy
Return policy applies across all Lucky Golf products. Customer-facing return policy is published on luckywedges.com. The below is captured here as the source of truth for copy that references return terms.
General return rules (all products)
Returns must be initiated within the applicable return window for the product category.
Refunds are issued only after the returned item is received and inspected.
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs.
Original shipping fees are non-refundable.
Items returned outside stated conditions may be subject to partial refunds or return denial.
Customized items are final sale and not eligible for return unless deemed defective.
How to start a return
Apparel and gear: through the Returns Portal or the instructions in the order confirmation.
Golf clubs: all club returns require approval before being sent back. Contact Customer Support through website chat or at support@luckygolf.com.
Golf clubs — 60-day return policy
All golf club returns require approval before return, require photo verification prior to approval, and are refunded only after physical inspection.

Putters: may be used during the 60-day period. To qualify for a refund, must be returned in brand-new condition with no face marks, sole wear, scratches, dents, chips, bag chatter, or grip wear. Modifications (regripping, bending, cutting, altering) are not permitted. Wear or damage may result in partial refund or return denial.

Wedges: only one wedge per set may be opened and tested, on a turf mat or other non-abrasive surface. Must be returned in brand-new condition with no groove wear, face marks, sole wear, or cosmetic or structural damage. All other wedges in the set must remain factory sealed. Significant use or damage may result in partial refund or return denial.

Irons: only the 7-iron may be removed from factory plastic and tested on a turf mat or non-abrasive surface. All other irons must remain factory sealed. Entire set must be returned in brand-new condition. Significant use or damage may result in partial refund or return denial.

Drivers, hybrids, and woods: must appear completely unused, with no visible signs of wear or damage and no modifications. Significant use or damage may result in partial refund or no refund depending on condition.
Apparel — 30-day return policy
Must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition.
All original tags must be attached.
No visible wear or damage: no makeup or deodorant stains, odors, pet hair, stretching, or laundering.
Items returned without tags will have a $7 fee deducted from the refund.
Returns requested after 30 days of delivery will not be accepted.
Gear and accessories — 30-day return policy
Must be returned in unused condition.
Items showing wear, damage, or use are not eligible for return.
Defective items may be eligible for replacement or refund upon inspection.
Customized items
All customized products are final sale and not eligible for return or refund unless deemed defective upon inspection.
Non-returnable conditions (all products)
Returns will not be accepted if the item shows visible wear or damage beyond stated conditions, has been modified, is outside the return window, is missing required approval or documentation, or is returned without authorization.
