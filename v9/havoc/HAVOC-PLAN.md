## What we already know (product guide)

- Adjustable adapter, 8 settings (changes loft, lie and face angle together). Chart still coming from Wenst.
- Weights: one 9g back + two 3g (toe, heel), swappable with the wrench. Heavy weight to heel = draw, to toe = fade.
- 45", D2, black rubber grip
- Stock shaft: Wenst, Stiff (Regular orderable). Upgrade: KBS PGW CB 60, low launch / low spin, Stiff and X.
- Box: white headcover, black torque wrench, driver box
- USGA: NOT on the conforming list, not submitted. Never say conforming/legal until it is.

## Adjustments, macro vs micro
- Adapter = macro. Each setting moves loft (±1.5° to ±2° on most drivers) and face angle with it (more loft closes the face, less opens it), plus lie. Changes launch, spin and start direction.
- Weights = micro. Shift draw/fade bias, spin and forgiveness. Independent tests show modest, inconsistent effects, so claims stay small.
- Page names one "start here" setting (stock loft, neutral weights).
