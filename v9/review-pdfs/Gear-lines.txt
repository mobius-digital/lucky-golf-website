Gear item lines (one per gear page)

cover-blade: Cut for a blade head, white with the clover on it. The Eclipse Blade ships without a cover, and this keeps the face off your irons.

cover-mallet: Shaped for a mallet head, white with the clover. The Eclipse Mallet ships without a cover, and a mallet's the one that takes the most bag chatter.

cover-driver: White with the stripes and the clover. It fits a driver or a fairway wood, and the top of the bag stops looking like a rental.

glove-tour: The clover on the back of the hand, in four sizes. Pick the size you already wear, and if you're between two, go down: a glove stretches and never shrinks back.

grip-clover: Tour velvet style, soft and tacky, with the clover on it. Midsize and Jumbo cost a little more because there's more rubber in them.

grip-clover-pink: Tour velvet style, soft and tacky, in pink and the Lady size, which is the same grip in a smaller diameter.

grip-putter-oversized: An oversized putter grip in four thicknesses. Bigger quiets the hands in the stroke, which is the whole reason people go oversized.

grip-putter-stock: The grip that comes on an Eclipse, sold on its own, for when yours has worn smooth and you want it back the way it arrived.
