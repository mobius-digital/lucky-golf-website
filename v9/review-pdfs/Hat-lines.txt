The 10 hat lines (one per hat page)

White | Gold Classic Hat: White, with the wordmark in gold across the front. It goes with every polo in the rack.

It's Better To Be Lucky Hat, white: The line we put on everything, on a white patch hat. If somebody asks what it means, you've already won the conversation.

Cream Upside Down Hat: Cream, with the wordmark flipped. People notice it a beat later than they expect to.

It's Better To Be Lucky Hat, black: Black, with the line on a patch across the front. The joke, minus the brightness.

Tan Cursive Hat: Tan with the script across the front. Easy on the eyes, and it calms down the loud prints.

Black | Gold Classic Hat: Black with the wordmark in gold. The Carver 02 Black of hats.

White/Black Upside Down Hat: White crown, black brim, wordmark upside down. All the contrast without going to a black hat.

White Upside Down Hat: All white, wordmark flipped. Clean from the front, and it keeps the sun off just the same.

White Cursive Hat: White with the script in black. The easiest one here to wear off the course.

Baby Blue Cursive Hat: Baby blue, with the script tonal across the front. Nothing else we make is this colour.
