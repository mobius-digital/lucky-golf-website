Shipping

Out the door in a day or two

Orders over $198 ship free, which two wedges gets you to exactly.

When does my order ship?

Orders leave us in one to two business days. You'll get tracking the moment the label is made, not when we get round to it.

When is shipping free?

At $198. Two Carvers land on it exactly, which is not an accident.

Do you ship outside the US?

Rates and times depend on where you are, so they're calculated at checkout before you pay. Duties and import charges are set by your country, not by us.

Can I change the address?

If it hasn't shipped, yes. Email us the order number and the right address and we'll catch it. Once it's with the carrier it's out of our hands.

Returns

Sixty days on clubs, thirty on the rest

Take the club to the course. That's the point of the 60 days, and it's why the conditions below exist.

How does the 60-day club return work?

Start it with us before you send anything back: club returns need approval and a couple of photos first, and the refund goes out after the club is back with us and inspected. Return shipping is on you, and the original shipping fee isn't refunded.

Can I actually hit it?

Yes, that's the deal, with one condition per club type. A putter can be rolled during the 60 days and has to come back with no face marks, sole wear, scratches, dents, chips, bag chatter or grip wear. With wedges, one wedge in the set can be opened and tested on a turf mat or another non-abrasive surface, with no groove wear or face marks, and the rest stay factory sealed. Regripping, bending or cutting a club ends the return.

What about a polo or a hat?

Thirty days. Apparel comes back unworn and unwashed with the original tags on: no stains, odours, pet hair or stretching. Tags missing costs $7 off the refund. Gear and accessories come back unused inside the same 30 days.

What can't come back?

Anything customised is final sale unless it turns up defective. Beyond that: visible wear or damage past the conditions above, modified clubs, anything outside its window, and anything sent back without approval.

Something arrived damaged.

Email us a photo the day it lands. Defective goes to replacement or refund after we look at it, and you're not paying to ship a broken thing back to us.

The clubs

Which one you actually need

Five clubs, and no salesperson standing over you. Here's the short version.

Which wedge should I start with?

Whatever gap hurts most. If you're carrying a pitching wedge and nothing else, a 56 covers the most ground in one club. If you already have a sand wedge, a 60 gives you the shot over the bunker that a 56 can't hold.

Gold or black?

Same wedge underneath. The black runs ten dollars more because of the finish, and it's the quieter one at address. Pick the one you want to look down at.

Blade or mallet putter?

Blade if your stroke arcs, mallet if it runs straight back and through. If you don't know which you do, the mallet is the more forgiving guess.

Are these actually as good as the big names?

Same materials and the same engineering, forged rather than cast, without a tour contract or a retail shelf built into the price. You have 60 days to disagree with us on the course.

Apparel

Sizing, fabric and the same price in every size

Thirteen prints, two collars, one price. The 3XL costs what the small costs.

How do the polos fit?

Tailored athletic: it follows the shoulders and tapers slightly through the body, so it sits closer than a boxy golf polo without holding the swing. If you wear a large in a performance polo, a large here is right. Between sizes, size up.

What are they made of?

88/12 poly-spandex, under 180gsm, four-way stretch, moisture-wicking, wrinkle-resistant, UPF 50+. Machine wash, no special handling.

Classic collar or Blade?

The Blade drops the buttons and the placket, so there's nothing at your throat to sit crooked by the ninth. Everything under the collar is identical.

Do the hats come in sizes?

One size, five-panel snapback, so you set it where you like it. Moisture-wicking sweatband and laser-cut ventilation in all ten.

Orders

After you hit buy

Nothing clever happens here. It's a confirmation, a tracking number and a box.

Can I change or cancel an order?

If it hasn't shipped, email us straight away with the order number. Once the carrier has it, treat it as a return.

Where's my tracking?

It goes to the email on the order as soon as the label is made. If it isn't there, check the spam folder, then email us and we'll resend it.

Do you restock sold-out lofts and sizes?

Yes. Tap the sold-out chip on the product page and we'll email you when that one is back. We don't email you about anything else.

Do you take returns on a gift?

Same windows, same conditions, and we'll deal with whoever contacts us. You don't need the buyer standing there.
