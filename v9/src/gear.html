Before you add it

The bits people get wrong

Grips and covers are cheap to change and change how the bag feels more than most people expect.

Does a club come with a head cover?

No. Wedges, putters and the hybrid all ship without one, which is why the covers live here. If you want your putter protected in the bag, add one.

Will the blade cover fit my putter?

The blade cover is cut for a blade head and the mallet cover for a mallet. Match the shape of the head, not the brand on it.

What size grip do I need?

The clover grips come in more than one size, and the size changes the price, which is why you see a range. Pick the one you play now if you know it. If you do not, standard is the safe answer.

Can I send gear back?

Thirty days, unused. A grip that has been fitted to a club has been used, so decide before it goes on the shaft.

The last word

Ten dollars of better

A fresh grip is the cheapest thing in golf that you can actually feel. Mags has strong views on this and no hands.
