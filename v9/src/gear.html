Before you add it

Get the fit right

A grip or a cover changes how the bag feels more than you'd expect.

Does a club come with a head cover?

No. Our clubs ship without one, which is why the covers live here.

Will the blade cover fit my putter?

The blade cover's cut for a blade head and the mallet cover for a mallet. Match the shape of the head, not the brand on it.

What size grip do I need?

The clover grips come in Standard, Midsize and Jumbo, and the bigger ones cost a little more. If you know the size you play, pick that. If you don't, Standard is the safe call.

Can I send gear back?

Thirty days, unused. A grip that's been fitted to a club counts as used, so decide before it goes on the shaft.
