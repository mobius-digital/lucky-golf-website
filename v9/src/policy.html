Policy

Refunds and returns

The short version is on the questions page. This is the full text, and it is what we go by.

Last updated 20 September 2026

In one line: sixty days on clubs, thirty on everything else. Putters can be rolled and one wedge per set can be tested on a mat, as long as everything comes back in the condition described below.

The windows

Returns must be started inside the window for that category: sixty days from delivery for golf clubs, thirty days from delivery for apparel, gear and accessories. Refunds are issued once the item is back with us and has been inspected. Return shipping is the customer's, and the original shipping fee is not refunded.

Golf clubs, sixty days

Every club return needs approval before it is sent back, and photo verification before that approval. Refunds follow physical inspection.

  • Putters may be used during the sixty days. To be refunded they must come back in brand-new condition: no face marks, sole wear, scratches, dents, chips, bag chatter or grip wear.
  • Wedges: one wedge per set may be opened and tested, on a turf mat or another non-abrasive surface. It must come back with no groove wear, face marks, sole wear or cosmetic and structural damage. Every other wedge in the set stays factory sealed.
  • Hybrids and woods must appear completely unused, with no visible wear or damage and no modifications.
  • Irons: only the 7 iron may come out of its factory plastic and be tested on a turf mat or non-abrasive surface. The rest stay sealed and the whole set comes back in brand-new condition.

Modifying a club ends the return: regripping, bending, cutting or altering it in any way. Wear or damage beyond the conditions above may mean a partial refund or a declined return.

Apparel, thirty days

Apparel comes back unworn, unwashed and in original condition with all original tags attached. No makeup or deodorant marks, odors, pet hair, stretching or laundering. Items returned without tags have $7 deducted from the refund. Requests made more than thirty days after delivery are not accepted.

Gear and accessories, thirty days

Unused, inside thirty days. Anything showing wear, damage or use is not eligible. Defective items may be replaced or refunded after inspection.

Customized items

Anything customized is final sale and cannot be returned or refunded unless it is found to be defective on inspection.

What we can't accept

A return will be declined if the item shows wear or damage beyond the conditions above, has been modified, falls outside its window, is missing the required approval or documentation, or arrives without authorization.

Starting a return

Apparel and gear go through the returns portal, or the instructions in your order confirmation. Clubs need approval first: message us through the chat on any page or email support@luckygolf.com with your order number and a couple of photos, and we'll set it up.

Something arrived damaged? Send a photo the day it lands, and defective items get a replacement or a refund once we've taken a look.

The last word

“It's better to be lucky.”

Sixty days to make up your mind on clubs, thirty on apparel.
