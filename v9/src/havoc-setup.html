Setup guide

Set up your Havoc.

Two minutes with the wrench and you can change how high it flies and which way it bends. Watch it done once, then use the charts below.

1 · The big move

The adapter.

Two rings, eight settings. The top ring sets the loft. The bottom ring sets the lie: N is standard, D stands the club up so the ball starts more left.

Lie−1•+1+2
N9.5°Lower10.5°Stock11.5°Higher12.5°Highest
D9.5°Starts left10.5°Starts left11.5°Starts left12.5°Starts left
+1 / +2
Higher flight and more spin. The face also sits a touch closed, so the ball tends to go a little left.
−1
Lower flight and less spin. The face sits a touch open, so the ball tends to go a little right.
D
Stands the club up. The ball starts further left. For the right miss only.

The face moves a little every time the loft does, so check where you're aiming after any change.

2 · The small move

The weights.

One 9g and two 3g, all swappable with the same wrench. Wherever the heavy one sits, it nudges the shape. Get close with the adapter first, then fine-tune here.

Back
The most forgiving spot, and the most stable flight. How it ships.
Heel
Helps the face close through impact. Less slice, more draw.
Toe
Helps the face stay open through impact. Less hook, more fade.

3 · Fix your miss

Same miss every time? Start here.

One change at a time, then hit a few. If it's better, stop. If it isn't, try the next one on the row.

There's no "fade" setting on the ring. For a ball that keeps going left, −1 and the toe weight are your fade tools.

4 · The wrench

Five steps. Two minutes.

  1. Loosen the screw on the sole by the heel. Lefty loosey.
  2. Pull the head off the shaft.
  3. Turn the sleeve until your setting lines up with the mark on the head.
  4. Slide the head back on.
  5. Tighten until the wrench clicks. Then stop. Weights come out and go in the same way, same click.

Questions

The stuff people ask.

Will changing it hurt the club?

No. That's what it's built for. Tighten until the wrench clicks and stop there.

Does changing the loft change where it aims?

A little. Adding loft closes the face a touch and taking it off opens it a touch, so check your aim after a switch.

Should I change the adapter or the weights first?

The adapter. It makes the bigger change. The weights fine-tune a shot you've already got close.

Where's the wrench?

In the box, with the white headcover.

Is there a lefty Havoc?

Not yet. Get on the list and tick the lefty box.
