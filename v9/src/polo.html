The polo

Cut to play in, good enough to keep on after

Here's the thing about most golf polos: they're cut like a box, and you feel it at the top of the backswing when the whole shirt drags across your shoulders. This one doesn't do that. Tailored athletic cut, 88/12 poly-spandex, four-way stretch, and it moves when you do.

You'll notice the UPF 50+ and the moisture-wicking in August and then never think about either again, which is how that stuff is supposed to work. It also comes out of a bag without looking like it spent the morning in one.

Every print in the range is this same shirt underneath, so there's nothing to weigh up on fabric. Pick the one you actually want to look down at.

How it fits

Tailored athletic. It follows the shoulders and tapers slightly through the body, so it sits closer than a boxy golf polo without restricting the swing. Between sizes, or want room across the chest? Size up.

Refund policy

Thirty days, tags on

Apparel comes back within thirty days of delivery, unworn, unwashed and in original condition with all original tags attached.

No makeup or deodorant marks, odours, pet hair, stretching or laundering. A return without its tags has $7 deducted from the refund.

Requests after thirty days aren't accepted. Clubs get sixty days; apparel doesn't.

Wrong size

Size is the most common reason anything comes back, which is why the size guide is worth thirty seconds before you order. Refunds are issued after the item is received and inspected. Return shipping is yours, and original shipping isn't refunded.
