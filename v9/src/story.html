Why we exist

Great clubs got too expensive. We took it personally

We started Lucky for two reasons: good golf gear cost way too much, and golf itself forgot it was supposed to be fun.

The industry's playbook is simple: sponsor a tour pro and add it to the price. The steel didn't change. The price did. We're not playing that game. Same materials as the big names, same engineering, none of the markup. Sold direct, so the saving lands in your pocket instead of a sponsorship deal.

And we made them gold, because standing out is the whole point. Or black, if you'd rather they notice up close.

Our story

We built the clubs we wanted

We loved golf. We didn't love the bill, and we really didn't love how seriously golf was taking itself. So in 2018 we looked into why.

Turns out the big brands use the same materials anyone can buy. The rest of the price tag is tour sponsorships and retail shelf space. So we made our own: one gold wedge, built right, priced honestly. It hit.

The blade putter followed, then the mallet, then the hybrid, then the apparel. Same plan since day one: build it right, price it fair, have fun with it.
