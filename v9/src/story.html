Why we exist

Great clubs got too expensive. We took it personally

We started Lucky for two reasons: good golf gear cost way too much, and golf itself forgot it was supposed to be fun.

The industry's playbook is simple: charge more, sponsor a tour pro, call it innovation. The steel didn't change. The price did. We're not playing that game. Same materials as the big names, same engineering, none of the markup. Sold direct, so the saving lands in your pocket instead of a sponsorship deal.

And we made the clubs gold, because standing out is the whole point. Golf has enough gear that whispers.

What we do

Same steel as the big names. Different math

Our stuff is built from the same high-end materials the big names use, engineered for real performance, and finished in gold so it doesn't look like anyone else's bag.

We cut out the middlemen, the retail markup and the hype. No buzzword launches, no four-figure price tags. Every club gets a 60-day test drive: roll it, hit it, take it to the course. If it doesn't earn its spot in the bag, send it back.

2018 to now

How a gold wedge turned into all this

We loved golf. We didn't love the bill, and we really didn't love how seriously golf was taking itself. So we made our own.

  1. 2018

    The gold wedge

    One wedge, in gold. Same steel as the big names, none of the markup. Lucky starts here.

  2. 2019

    The Blade putter

    CNC-milled from solid stainless, quiet off the face. Turns out people wanted more than a wedge.

  3. 2020

    Wedges, version two

    The original, refined and re-released. The gold wedge wasn't a fluke.

  4. 2023

    The Mallet putter

    More forgiveness, for the stroke most golfers actually have.

  5. 2024

    The hybrid

    Titanium with a flex-channel face, for the gap your long irons never covered.

  6. July 2025

    Somebody hit the gas

    Lucky changed hands. Nothing happened to the clubs or the prices. Everything happened to the calendar.

  7. May 2026

    The polos drop

    The clubs got a wardrobe. Same rule: look good, priced fair.

  8. July 2026
