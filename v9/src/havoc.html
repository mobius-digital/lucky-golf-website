What it's made of

A 460cc head in cast 6-4 titanium, with an SP700 titanium face that's thicker in the centre and thinner toward the edges. 10.5° of loft, a 58° lie and a square face out of the box. Matte gold, with Lucky filled in black on the sole. Built to 45 inches at D2.

What's in the box

The head on the shaft you picked, our black rubber grip, a white headcover and the black torque wrench, all in the driver box.

Shipping and returns

Orders leave Long Beach, California in 1 to 2 business days. Every club gets sixty days from delivery. Talk to us before anything ships back, because club returns need our OK and a few photos first. Return shipping's on you.

What's under the gold.

  1. 01

    SP700 face, thick in the middle, thin at the edges

    A springier titanium right where the ball hits. Catch it flush and it comes off hot. Catch it on the toe and the drive that should've died short still gets out there.

  2. 02

    12″ bulge and roll

    The face curves heel to toe and top to bottom. Hit one off the heel or the toe and the curve pulls it back toward the fairway instead of letting it run off.

  3. 03

    460cc of titanium

    The biggest head the rules allow. More face to hit, a confident look at address, and the mishits stay a lot closer to the good ones.

  4. 04

    8 settings, 3 weights

    Set the flight yourself. Higher, lower, draw, fade. No fitting appointment, no second driver. How it works
