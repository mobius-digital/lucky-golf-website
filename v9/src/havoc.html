Set it up

Big moves first. Then the small stuff.

Hit it the way it comes for a round or two. If the ball keeps doing the same thing, the adapter is where you fix it. Once that's close, the weights fine-tune it.

Eight settings: four lofts on the top ring, two lies on the bottom one. More loft gets the ball up and adds spin. D stands the club up a touch, which helps the ball start left (for a righty). The face angle moves a little with every loft change, so re-check your aim after a switch.

Big move

The adapter

Loft and lie. This changes how high it flies and where it starts. If you're missing in the same spot every time, start here.

  • Ballooning or spinning too much: go down a click.
  • Low and dying: go up a click.
  • Starting right of where you aimed: try D.
Small move

The weights

One 9g and two 3g. Swap where the heavy one sits to nudge the shape.

  • Heavy one in the back: the most forgiving spot. How it ships.
  • Heavy one toward the heel: helps turn it over (draw).
  • Heavy one toward the toe: helps hold it up (fade).
  1. Loosen the screw on the sole by the heel. Lefty loosey.
  2. Pull the head off the shaft.
  3. Turn the adapter until your setting lines up with the mark.
  4. Slide the head back on.
  5. Tighten until the wrench clicks. Then stop.
