You smoked the drive and came up a touch short with the approach. Now it's a tight lie, ten yards to the green, and a putt for par if you get this close. Then the club hits the turf first, a chunk of grass goes further than the ball, and you're still in the same spot, lying one more.

That's the chili dip, and every golfer has one in them. The good news is it has one cause, which makes it one of the easier misses in golf to fix.

Why it happens

Every swing has a low point, the spot where the club bottoms out. On a good chip that spot is just in front of the ball, so the club catches the ball first and then brushes the turf. On a chili dip the low point is behind the ball, and the ground gets hit first.

Three habits move it back there. Weight hanging on the back foot is the big one. Trying to help the ball into the air with a scooping flick of the hands is next. Then there's the nervous one: slowing the club down right before impact, which lets the clubhead drop and dig.

You don't need a new swing. You need the bottom of the one you've got to move forward.

The fix

  • Weight forward, and leave it there. Set most of your weight on your front foot at address and keep it there through the shot.
  • Ball in the middle or a touch back. Hands just ahead of it, so the shaft leans toward the target.
  • Turn, don't flick. Let your chest rotate through the shot and keep the club moving at the same speed to the finish. The loft gets the ball up. That's its job, not yours.
  • Use the sole. A wedge is built to glide on its bottom. Let the sole brush the grass instead of driving the leading edge into it.

The towel drill

Lay a towel on the practice green about four inches behind your ball. Chip without touching the towel. If you're hitting it, your low point's still behind the ball. Ten minutes of this and the club starts finding the ball first on its own.

Once that's working, draw a line in the fringe, set up with the ball on it and try to make every brush mark land on the target side of the line. When they do, the chili dip's gone.
