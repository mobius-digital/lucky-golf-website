# Takomo's new putter (Lumo) vs our club pages

Checked live 2026-10-08. Takomo launched the **Lumo M1 (mallet)** and **Lumo B1 (blade)** on Oct 7. $219, Black DLC +$20, pre-order.

## What they actually do

- **Two pages, not one.** The product page is short. The tech story lives on a separate launch page (`/pages/lumo-putters`) linked from the home page.
- **Product page order:** buy box (15 photos + 1 silent looping video, "Watch product video" pop-up, length "Help me choose" chart) → sticky bar that doubles as page nav (Description / Specs / Reviews / Buy) → "Others also viewed" → "The full breakdown" (copy + their launch video, quick facts: type, for who, handicap range, care guide PDF) → spec table with Clubs / Shafts / Grips tabs → 2 YouTube creator reviews → written reviews.
- **Launch page:** video hero → story → the two models → 3 lifestyle photos → **3 tech cards, each a short muted video that plays when you scroll to it** (heel shaft lined up with the centre of gravity, milled from one piece, crisp feel) → mallet vs blade table → "what is low torque" explainer → grip + headcover → FAQ.
- **The "animation" is just video.** No 3D, no Lottie, no scroll-scrubbing. Muted MP4s that start when on screen. We can do the same with Higgsfield clips.
- **Numbers live in the spec table, not the story:** head weights by length (362/357/352 g), 14 mm onset, shaft 124 g, grip 76 g.

## What I'd build next (needs you)

1. **3 short silent clips per club** (5 to 8 s, vertical or 4:5): Carver = face/grooves close-up, a chip checking up, the sole. Putters = milling close-up, address view, a putt rolling. These drop straight into "What's inside".
2. **Putter tech facts we don't have:** toe hang, face milling pattern, offset/onset, centre of gravity. Takomo's whole Lumo pitch is built on these.
