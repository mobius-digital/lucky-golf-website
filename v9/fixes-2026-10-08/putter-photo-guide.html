Putter photo guide

Four phone photos per putter: one set for the Blade, one for the Mallet. Ten minutes total.

1Toe hang: balance it on one finger

  1. Hold the putter flat, face pointing up, and rest the shaft on one finger.
  2. Let go with your other hand and let the head settle on its own.
  3. Take the photo from behind the grip, looking straight down the shaft at the head.

Tells golfers which stroke it suits. I'll read the angle off the photo.

2Offset: straight down from above

  1. Set the putter down like you're about to putt, sole flat, shaft leaning back like normal.
  2. Hold the phone flat over the head and shoot straight down. Get the whole head and the bottom of the shaft in the frame.

Shows whether the shaft sits ahead of the face (offset) and by how much.

3Face-on: phone on the table

  1. Same setup as photo 2.
  2. Stand the phone on the table, camera at ball height, pointing straight at the face.

Shows the neck and how the shaft goes into the head.

4Weight: on a kitchen scale

  1. Lay the whole putter across the scale (balance it if it wants to tip).
  2. Photo of the number.

A buyer said the Blade weighed 760 g in total. This settles it.
