What it's for

The only part of the club you touch

A worn grip makes you hold on tighter, and holding on tighter is the fastest way to ruin a swing. Most golfers go years without changing them.

These cost under fifteen dollars. Change them.

Sizing

Standard, midsize, jumbo

Bigger grips quieten the hands; smaller ones let them work. If your hands are large or you tend to flip through impact, go up a size.

Price goes up with size, which is why the buy box repaints when you choose.
