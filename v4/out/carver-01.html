Two grinds

Square it up, or open it up

Grind is the one choice on this page that changes how the club plays. The K is the safe pick and comes in every loft. The S is for golfers who lay the face open. If your chips don't stop, either one fixes that.

K grind

No surprises

A wider, flatter sole with a standard leading edge. Set up square, take your divot, get the same result every time. Built for standard lies: fairway, rough, fringe.

All six lofts · 50 to 60
S grind

Versatility

A narrower, relieved sole with a reshaped leading edge, so the face opens without the bounce fighting you. Built for tight lies, bunker shots and open-face lobs.

Three lofts · 52 · 56 · 60

The Black comes in the K grind only. The S lives on the Gold.
